Our courtyard

Seated under our own roof, not someone else's food court

JD's Kitchen runs out of a timber-roofed, open-sided dining hall in Parambath, Paloramala — built for the kind of unhurried, plate-sharing lunches Malabar biryani is meant for. Warm pendant lighting, wicker-and-steel seating, and enough room to not feel rushed.

We're best known for our beef and chicken biryani and our chicken mandi, but the evening snack spread and the outdoor tea counter have quietly become just as loved by regulars passing through Kozhikode.

"Ample parking space, well maintained, and spacious." — a regular's take on what sets the courtyard apart.
